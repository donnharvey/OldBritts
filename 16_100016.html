Commando Swept Back Exhaust Pipes
Pre MK3 and MK3
by Viking

The current price for the Pre Mk3 set of pipes (part # 16-100016) is DROPPED, and the MK3 set of pipes, (part # 16-100017) is $0.00, as of 02/12/21.

This set of pictures shows the Commando swept back pipes. The pipes are designed to mount to the stock Roadster mounting brackets when peashooter mufflers are used. The Pre MK3 pipes will not fit around the MK3 primary case, but the MK3 pipes will work for both MK3 and Pre MK3 bikes. When these pipes are mounted to a MK3, you will need to remove the split collets (part # 06-5260) and replace the two spherical seating washers (part # 06-5259) with two Squash Washers (part # 06-3253) $0.95.
