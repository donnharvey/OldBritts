NORTON, Rocker Spindle Alignment Tool

The current price for this tool (part # 06-7185) is $40.84, as of 02/12/21.

This tool consists of the lever arm and the alignment rod. For Commando engines with high pressure oil feed, fit the rocker spindles with the oil hole facing away from the center of the cylinder head. Atlas engines with return oil feed, fit the rocker spindles with the oil hole facing the center of the cylinder head.

This picture shows the tool coupled with a Commando rocker spindle ( 06-7924).
