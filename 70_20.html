All our bars are listed with the following measurements: W for end to end width, H for height from triple clamp to top of bar, and PB for pull back from mounting. The following pictures show how we measure the bars:

The W (for width) is measured from bar end to bar end.


The H (for height) is measured from the clamping surface (triple clamp) to the top of the bar.


The PB (for pull back) is measured from the front of the bar (where it is clamped to the triple clamp) to the end of the bar.
