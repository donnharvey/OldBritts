Commando Fuel Line Assembly, Mikuni Twin Carb, w Filters

This picture shows the fuel line for Commandos with a twin Mikuni carburetors.

The current price for this fuel line (part no. 06-5192/2M) is $86.00 as of 02/12/21.

Mounting instructions

With the Mikuni carburetor drawing fuel from the top of the float bowl, it becomes hard to attach a fuel line to the carburetor and both petcocks and still feed both carburetors. What we have come up with is a fuel line with two "T"s, one below the carbs and one on top of the carbs. The fuel goes from the petcocks through the filters to a "T" under the carbs, then up between the carbs to the second "T" and from there to the fuel intake on the carbs.
