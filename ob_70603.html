Gearbox Lockring Socket

Bottom side of the Andover Norton socket.

Top side of the Andover Norton socket.

Bottom side of the discontinued Old britts sockett.

Top side of the discontinued Old britts socket.

Socket showing how the lockring and lever body fit into the socket.

This socket is designed to fit around the lockring, providing a secure nonslip fitment. The top hex takes a 1" wrench. Our old home built tool, that we use to use, just had tabs to fit into the notches on the lock ring. This tool was hard to apply pressure to keep it in the notches and still loosen or tighten the lockring.

We have discontinued our socket for the time being since our price is more than twice the Andover Norton socket. We do believe our socket has a slightly better fit, but not worth the extra cost.

Socket in use with a 1" wrench.

The current price for the Andover Norton socket (part # NM23441) is $19.95.

We have discontinued manufacturing our socket and the old price for that socket (part # OB-70603) was $85.00.
Prices are current as of 02/12/21.
