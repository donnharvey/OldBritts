Excel Shouldered Rim

The prices are current as of 02/12/21, but do not include any shipping charges.

We currently list eight, 40 spoke shouldered Excel rims drilled for Commando hubs, but we can normally have Excel rims drilled to fit most standard hubs (36 or 40 spoke). You can order the rim drilled for your hub or you can send us your hub and we will have a rim drilled, laced and trued to your hub for $107.00 (not including the cost of the spokes or nipples).

The Commando rims are:
06-7712/A, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x19, Commando front or rear drum, $260.00.
06-7712/B, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x18, Commando front or rear drum, $260.00.
06-7712/D, Excel shouldered rim, WM4x18, Commando rear drum, $225.00.
06-1951/A, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x19, Commando front disc, $260.00.
06-1951/C, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x18, Commando front disc, $240.00.
06-6119/B, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x19, MK3 rear disc, $225.00.
06-6119/C, Excel shouldered rim, WM3x18, MK3 rear disc, $240.00.
06-6119/D, Excel shouldered rim, WM4x18, MK3 rear disc, $225.00.

Note: Rim sizes - WM3 = 2.15" inside width, WM4 = 2.5" inside width.
The recommended tire sizes for a WM3 are: 4.00" to 4.25" or 100 to 110 Metric.
The recommended tire sizes for a WM4 are: 4.50" to 5.10" or 110 to 120 Metric.

These rims come with a clear anodized coating, are made in Japan, but as you can see from the above picture the lettering stamped on the rim is very small and is stamped between two spoke dimples on either side of the valve stem hole.

The first two pictures show the rim with its clear anodized coating and in the third picture the anodized rim is placed on top of an Excel rim that has had the coating removed. As you can see the polished rim with the coating removed is only slightly brighter than the anodized rim. We can have the coating removed and polished for DROPPED.


This last picture shows an Excel WM3 19" rim with the anodized coating removed, laced with polished stainless spokes and nipples to a stock Commando front disc hub.
