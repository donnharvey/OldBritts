Honda, The TT Winning Years

By Peter Kneale & Bill Snelling

"I here avow my intention that I will participate in the TT race and I proclaim with my fellow employees that I will pour all my energy and creative powers into winning." When Soichiro Honda penned those words in 1954, little did the world know the impact that Japanese technology and innovative engineering would have on its markets, especially in the field of motorcycling. From Mike Hailwood to Phillip McCallen, the world's finest riders have pitted their craft against the world's finest pure road-race circuit, in the Isle of Man, and won. This book chronicles the 95 TT wins that Honda has gained since 1961.

Soft bound. 8 1/2", x 8", 207 pages, Approximately 189 color and 46 black and white pictures.
