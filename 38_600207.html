Stepped Spacer for Lower Engine Mount, Set of 2

The current price for the set of two spacers (part # 38-600207) is $45.00, as of 02/12/21.

A set of spacers.

These spacers are used when using a MK3 engine cradle with Pre-MK3 crank cases. The bottom rear mounting bolt for Pre-MK3 crank cases takes a 5/16 bolt where the MK3 takes a 3/8 bolt, like the top two mounting bolts on all Commando crank cases. The spacer works as a washer and a sleeve to convert the lower mounting hole to accept a 5/16 bolt.

The spacers inserted into a MK3 engine cradle and the 5/16 bolt.
