Oil Filter Kit
To Convert Older Models

All prices are for one item and are current as of 02/12/21.

We stock two oil filter conversion kits:

  • The first kit uses all Andover Norton factory parts, (part # 06-4283, $119.60)
  • The other kit uses Gates oil lines, hard plastic spring protectors and a Baldwin oil filter, (part # 06-4283/G, $88.65) This is not a lesser quality kit from the first kit, just less expensive.

These oil filter conversion kits can be used to add an oil filter to pre-1972 Commandos and other bikes without an oil filer. This kit consists of the following parts:

  • One mounting head (06-3139), $46.87.
  • One tab washer (06-3142), $1.50.
  • Two 12" return oil lines, (06-3144, $0.00) or (06-3144/G, $5.00)
  • Four 3/8" hose clamps (06-3248), $0.65.
  • Two spring protectives, (06-3367, $13.25) or (063367/A, $13.25)
  • One retainer (06-3368), $2.65.
  • One 3 1/2" filter clamp (06-3369), $3.25.
  • One oil filter, (06-3371, $25.11) or (42-200002, $17.46)
  • Two 5/16 x 24, 3/4" bolts (14-0114), $0.56.


Please note: You can read how to mount this kit in our Filter Mounting Instructions on our Technical Articles page.
