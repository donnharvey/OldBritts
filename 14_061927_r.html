We offer three recondition services for your caliper:

  • Reconditioned with all new parts and stainless pistons (part # 06-1927/CR, $346.14)
  • Reconditioned with all new parts except the caliper plug, with stainless pistons (part # 06-1927/CRO, $355.44)
  • Reconditioned with all new stainless parts (part # 06-1927/CRS, $372.45)

We will recondition your caliper with all the parts and services described in our technical article Commando caliper rebuild instructions. If you are not going to upgrade your caliper to a stainless plug, after we remove your plug, and it is not damaged (functionally or cosmetically), we will clean and polish your old plug for reuse.

If you do not want to have your bike down while we recondition your caliper and we have a reconditioned caliper in stock, we will send you a reconditioned caliper, charge you a $255.00 deposit until we receive your old caliper back.
