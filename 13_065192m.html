Mounting instructions

With the Mikuni carburetor drawing fuel from the top of the float bowl, it becomes hard to attach a fuel line to the carburetor and both petcocks without kinking the fuel line. This becomes very critical with the clear line since that is the most flexible of the three lines. Our fuel lines are designed to be mounted as shown in the following pictures.
