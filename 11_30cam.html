Norton Cams

The following chart lists the specifications for the different Norton cams that are currently available. The first column is the cam reference number. Cam numbers starting with "560-" are Megacycle cams. The LIFT column is the cam lift in inches. The DUR column is the duration in degree of rotation from .040" lift and till .040" lift. The LOB column is the degree of the lobe center. The T.S. column is the tappet style (flat or 3" radius). The INT / EXH column is the intake open/close (first line) and exhaust (second line) open/close specifications. The last column, CLEAR is the recommended tappet running clearance, intake (first line) and exhaust (second line).
