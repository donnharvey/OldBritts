Viking Exhaust Components

Old Britts is an importer for Norton Exhaust systems from Bryant Manufacturing (Viking Exhausts). The Viking Exhaust components are manufactured in New Zealand and are the highest quality exhaust systems we have found.

We try to keep the products that replace the stock exhaust components in stock and want to apologize in advance if we are out of the products you desire. We can order the custom products, but cannot provide an accurate time frame for delivery and cannot guarantee the price due to exchange rates and shipping costs.

The normal exhaust pipes made for Nortons are made from 18 gauge (1.2mm) pipe wall thickness, where the Viking pipes are made from 16 gauge (1.6mm) pipe wall thickness, which means a longer lasting, stronger, better fitting exhaust pipe. The chrome along with the craftsmanship is exceptional. If you were to hold a set of Toga pipes (the best we sold up to now) along side a set of Viking pipes, you would see the obvious difference in quality control, exceptional finish and exceptional craftsmanship. The motto of Paul Bryant, the Managing Director for Bryant Manufacturing, is Engineering, Artistry and Craftsmanship and the Viking products live up to his motto.

Due to the exceptional quality of the Viking products, manufacturing costs are higher so they are priced higher than the other exhaust systems we sell. We feel that the higher price is well worth the much higher quality and longer product life.
