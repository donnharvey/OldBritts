Norvil Rotor

This set of pictures shows a Norvil rotor (14-000002/D, lightened) and the lightened rotor with the disc carrier mounted in the center of the rotor. This rotor comes undrilled (14-000002), is made of cast iron and is 11 1/2" in diameter which is the actual size of the original Norvil rotors. The drilling of the holes lightens each rotor by 1/2 pounds. See the information on the disc carrier (14_000003) for how the rotor mounts to the hub. Even though this setup has the appearance of a floating disc, it is not floating and does qualify for vintage racing.

This set of pictures show the undrilled rotor and the lightened Norvil discs mounted on the front wheel of a Commando. This is a two disc setup with matching Norvil sliders (13_200001) and AP Lockheed Calipers (14_000004).
