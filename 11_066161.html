Timing Cover MK3 (can be used for Pre-MK3)


MK3 Timming Cover.

This timing cover can be used to replace pre-MK3 timing covers. Andover Norton does not manufacture the pre-MK3 timing cover since the MK3 cover is an improvement over the pre-MK3 covers. The MK3 cover has an inspection cover to check the cam chain tension. This cover also has an anti-sumping valve built into the timing cover.
