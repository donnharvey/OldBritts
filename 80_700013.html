Bonnie: The Development History

of The Triumph Bonneville

By J.R. Nelson

This full story of the Triumph Bonneville has been completely updated with 16 extra pages of text and photos, plus a comprehensive appendix giving exact specs and major part numbers (or every year of production. Chronicles the Bonneville's development year-by-year, including highly detailed mechanical and cosmetic changes, right down to the color schemes. Enables the enthusiast or restorer to establish the correct specs for every Bonnie ever built. 2nd edition. Soft bound., 6 ½"x 9", 184 pages, 135 black and white illustrations.
