Swinging Arm Welch Plug, MK3

The current price for one plug (part # 06-6623) is $0.80, as of 02/12/21.

The welch plug comes new as a concaved solid washer and to install you place the plug into the end of the swinging arm, concave side facing inward, punch the center to flatten out the plug causing it to tightly seal the swinging arm end.
