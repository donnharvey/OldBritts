Seats and Seat Covers
for Nortons

The seats and seat covers that are manufactured in England for Old Britts are exact replicas of the original seats. The manufacturer of our seats bought up all patterns and equipment from the company that originally provided seats to the Norton factory. All seats have metal seat pans, except the Norvil and Fastback seats.

We have the ability to have custom seats manufactured (i.e., Different logo color, shorter seat pan, etc.). Contact us about the details for ordering a custom seat.

To order electronically: click on the Order button for the part you want to place in your shopping cart. In order for this to work, your browser must use frames, and you must start at the Old Britts Home Page.

We try to keep all information current, but regret that prices and availability are subject to change. Last updated 02/12/21
